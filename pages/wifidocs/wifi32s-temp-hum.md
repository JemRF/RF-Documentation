## Introduction
The S32 WiFi Sensor support an external 3 wire analog Pressure Sensor using a 3-ping JST 2.54 connector with +5VDC, analog in, and ground. The pressure sensor supported are for a 5 volt DC analog sensor from 50 PSI to 1000 PSI. There is setting to tell the S32 what the PSI limit of the pressure sensor is.

Optional internal and external temperature sensors can be added, as well as an Internal Temperature & Humidity Sensor.


## Quick Start
When purchased the S32 Sensor will have a 3 pin JST 2.54 connector installed and a 6 inch 3 wire with connector to connect to the external Pressure sensor.
